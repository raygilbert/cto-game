# It Worked in the Demo

**Play it:** https://raygilbert.github.io/cto-game/

A short, funny browser game about being a CTO. Quick to play, no sign-up.

## The game

You are the CTO of ScopeCreep, a small company that sells workflow software. You have one week, Monday to Friday. Each day brings a new problem, and usually someone has already promised something about it. You make one decision a day. On Friday, the board asks what your week cost.

- Each day arrives as email and team chat.
- Promises pile up on a note on your desk. Some come back on Friday.
- The board reads your week and tells you why it decided what it did.
- Three weeks to play: **The Enterprise Deal**, **The Acquisition** and **The Outage**.
- Optional: rehearse with Pat, the board chair, before Friday.
- Share your result, or send a friend a challenge link to the same week.

## How it works

Every outcome comes from written rules, not dice or a hidden score. Make the same choices and you get the same result. The debrief at the end shows the rules that applied.

## Privacy

No accounts, no tracking, no server. Your progress is saved only in your own browser.
